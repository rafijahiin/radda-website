Fifty years beside the mothers of Mirpur

Radda was founded to give the poorest families in Mirpur the same quality of care as anyone else. That is still our purpose.

Who we are
A mother and child in Mirpur deserve the same quality of care as anyone, anywhere.

Radda MCH-FP Centre is a non-profit health centre in Mirpur, Dhaka. MCH-FP stands for Maternal and Child Health and Family Planning, the work we have done since our first day.

We serve families on low incomes, many of whom live in informal settlements, at little or no cost. Our health workers run three permanent clinics with laboratories, satellite clinics in the community, and an immunization programme in partnership with the Government of Bangladesh.

We are governed by a Board of Trustees and funded by the government, partners and individual supporters.

Our history

From a Swedish project to a Bangladeshi institution

  1. 1974

    Founded in Mirpur

    Rädda Barnen, then Swedish Save the Children, opens the centre to bring mother and child health care to underserved families.

  2. 1974 to 1994

    Twenty years of growth

    Rädda Barnen runs, manages and almost fully funds the centre as its services and reach expand.

  3. 1994

    Handed over to Bangladesh

    With the permission of the Ministry of Health, full responsibility for the centre passes to local management.

  4. Today

    Serving thousands of families

    Three permanent clinics, community outreach and a paramedic institute, working with the government.
