Our story

Started by Rädda Barnen. Run by Bangladesh.

Radda began in 1974 as a project of Rädda Barnen, Swedish Save the Children, to bring mother and child health care to the poorest families of Mirpur. For twenty years Rädda Barnen ran and funded the centre.

In 1994, with the permission of the Ministry of Health, the centre passed into Bangladeshi hands. It has worked alongside the government ever since, under a volunteer Board of Trustees.
