Reproductive health

Pregnancy and after birth

Regular check-ups during pregnancy catch problems early and keep mother and baby safe. We see mothers through pregnancy and in the weeks after birth.

  • Antenatal check-ups
  • Tetanus toxoid (TT) vaccination
  • Postnatal care for mother and newborn
  • Vitamin A for breastfeeding mothers
  • Health and nutrition education
  • Advice for the whole family

Child health

Vaccines and child health

We give every child the government's Expanded Programme on Immunization (EPI) schedule, protecting against the main childhood diseases, including the Hepatitis B vaccine for babies under one year. We also offer additional vaccines for children and adults.

VaccineWho it is for
Government EPI scheduleBabies and young children
Hepatitis B (EPI)Babies under 1 year
Hepatitis A and BAll ages
HibBabies up to 1 year
TyphoidChildren up to 5 years
MMR, chickenpox, anti-rabiesAll ages

Please bring your child's vaccination card to every visit.

Community health

Family planning and community health

Every family has the right to decide when to have children. Our counsellors explain the options in private and help couples choose the method that suits them. Our teams also work in the community, through our static clinics and satellite clinics.

  • Contraceptives and counselling
  • STI and HIV/AIDS prevention awareness
  • Breastfeeding promotion
  • Community health education
  • Static and satellite clinics
  • Physiotherapy

Support services

Laboratory tests

Each of our three permanent clinics has its own laboratory, so most routine tests can be done during your visit, at an affordable cost.

  • Clinical pathology
  • Biochemistry
  • Haematology
  • Tests during pregnancy
