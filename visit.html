Support Radda

Help keep care within reach

Many of the families we serve could not pay for care anywhere else. Gifts from people and organisations help us keep pregnancy check-ups, vaccines and family planning affordable, and rebuild what was damaged in 2026.

To give, or to partner with us, please contact our office. We will share bank and mobile banking details and send a receipt for every gift.
